## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a sanctuary of empowerment, medical clarity, and clinical trust. Designed for individuals navigating breast health journeys, prevention routines, and clinical survivorship, the aesthetic avoids sterile hospital coldness and juvenile pastel tropes. Instead, it balances high-end editorial calm with modern digital healthcare rigor.

The visual direction merges **Minimalist Modern Healthcare** with **Soft Tactile Glass**:
- **Tone:** Dignified, supportive, medically authoritative, and gentle.
- **Visual Feel:** Airy, uncrowded canvases with warm mineral undertones, whisper-thin borders, and soft daylight gradients that diffuse tension.
- **Emotional Impact:** Reassurance without condescension; structural clarity that relieves cognitive fatigue during emotionally vulnerable states.

## Colors

The palette establishes emotional grounding through warm rose tones balanced by tranquil clinical blues and organic restorative mint accents.

- **Primary (`#E91E63` - Rose Pink):** The hero identity color for vital calls to action, active navigation tabs, and key progress indicators.
- **Deep Berry (`#9C1542`):** Accessible contrast anchor for primary text pairings, emphasized metrics, and focused interactive states over pink backgrounds.
- **Rose Tint (`#FCE4EC`):** Soft foundational background for selected states, badge fills, and contextual warning surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#3B82F6` - Soft Blue):** Informational highlights, clinical insights, and screening timelines.
- **Calming Sky (`#EFF6FF`):** Tinted container background for informational cards and educational segments.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981` - Accent Mint):** Wellness milestones, successful screening logs, completed modules, and positive health feedback.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F8F7F4` - Off-White):** A comforting, low-glare organic surface replacing harsh device white.
- **Surface Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-contrast elevate-card base.
- **Text & Hierarchy:** Text Dark (`#1F2937`) delivers AA/AAA legibility across all body text; Muted Slate (`#64748B`) handles metadata and contextual guidance.
- **Border Neutral (`#E2E8F0`):** Fine structure lines to preserve container edges without visual noise.

## Typography

The typography pairings unify warmth and clinical clarity. 

- **Headlines (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Soft, open geometric counters cultivate an approachable, modern voice without compromising authority. Headings maintain a snug negative letter-spacing for refined editorial polish.
- **Body & Content (`Inter`):** Selected for its industry-leading legibility, neutral stroke distribution, and comprehensive x-height, ensuring reading comfort during extensive medical guides and diagnosis roadmaps.
- **Labels & Microcopy (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Medium to semibold weights with slight positive tracking maintain high legibility on compact mobile screens, badges, and status chips.

## Layout & Spacing

A 4-column layout governs mobile interfaces, shifting to an 8-column layout for tablets and a constrained 12-column system for wide-screen clinical portals (max content width: 1120px).

- **Vertical Rhythm:** Relies on strict 8pt base increments, with 4pt sub-increments for fine component pairings (e.g., input icons, micro labels).
- **Breathing Room:** Educational modules and self-examination guides enforce generous bottom padding (`space-xl` to `space-2xl`) to avoid sensory overload and thumb-occlusion over floating action elements.
- **Safe Margins:** Standard 16px (`1rem`) edge gutters ensure edge gestures remain comfortable on bezel-less modern devices.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows, opting instead for **soft atmospheric layering** and **tonal containment**.

- **Level 0 (Flat):** Off-White (`#F8F7F4`) background.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) surface combined with a delicate boundary of `1px solid #E2E8F0` and an ambient glow: `0px 4px 16px -2px rgba(156, 21, 66, 0.03), 0px 2px 6px -1px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive & Floating Elements):** Active cards, bottom sheets, and floating guidance widgets apply a rose-tinted soft elevation: `0px 10px 24px -4px rgba(233, 30, 99, 0.08), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):** Dialogs use a deep berry backdrop scrim (`#1F2937` at 40% opacity with a `4px` blur), supporting an elevated surface with `0px 20px 32px -8px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.12)`.
- **Gradients:** Restrained accent gradients run diagonally (135°) from Rose Tint (`#FCE4EC`) to Calming Sky (`#EFF6FF`) on high-light milestone panels and progress cards.

## Shapes

Organic, friendly geometries are key to calming user anxiety. The system sets a baseline standard of medium-rounded curvature across cards and interactive containers, shifting up to 16px–24px on hero components:

- **Interactive Primary Elements:** 12px (`0.75rem`) to 16px (`1rem`) for inputs, buttons, and action panels.
- **Content Cards & Guidance Sections:** 16px (`1rem`) to 20px (`1.25rem`) for standard cards; 24px (`1.5rem`) for hero guidance tiles.
- **Status Badges, Chips & Avatars:** Fully rounded pill forms (`9999px`) to maintain a soft visual identity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Primary Rose (`#E91E63`) with white text, 14px vertical padding, 24px horizontal padding, 16px corner radius. In hover/active states, transitions seamlessly to Deep Berry (`#9C1542`).
- **Secondary Action:** Calming Sky (`#EFF6FF`) fill with Soft Blue (`#3B82F6`) text and 1px border of `#BFDBFE`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary Action:** Transparent background with Muted Slate (`#64748B`) text, switching to Deep Berry on pressed state.

### Cards & Guidance Modules
- Background is Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with 20px corner radius, framed by a 1px border of `#E2E8F0`. 
- Header banners inside guidance cards can feature a subtle linear gradient (`#FCE4EC` to `#EFF6FF`). Internal padding standard is 20px (`1.25rem`).

### Chips & Tags
- **Category & Topic Tags:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), 6px vertical padding, 12px horizontal padding. Background `#F8F7F4`, text `#64748B`, 1px border `#E2E8F0`.
- **Active / Selected Filter:** Rose Tint (`#FCE4EC`) background, Deep Berry (`#9C1542`) text, 1px border `#F48FB1`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Height of 48px to allow easy thumb-tap targeting. 
- Background `#FFFFFF` with 1px border `#E2E8F0` and 12px radius. 
- Focused state features a crisp 1.5px border in Primary Rose (`#E91E63`) backed by a 3px soft focus ring (`rgba(233, 30, 99, 0.15)`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radio Buttons)
- 20px geometric size with 6px border radius (checkbox) or circular format (radio).
- Unchecked: 1.5px border `#CBD5E1` with `#FFFFFF` fill.
- Checked: `#E91E63` fill with high-contrast white checkmark icon.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Self-Exam Routine Guide Card:** A dual-surface step card featuring a tactile illustrated zone, progress indicator in Accent Mint (`#10B981`), and clear, step-by-step typography.
- **Doctor Consultation Prep Note:** An expandable checklist card with Calming Sky (`#EFF6FF`) accents and high-contrast typography designed for quick reference in clinic environments.
- **Symptom Tracker Timeline:** Vertical rhythm bar featuring a continuous line in `#E2E8F0` connected by status nodes that turn `#E91E63` or `#10B981` upon milestone completion.
